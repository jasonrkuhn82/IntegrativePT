Trigger point dry needling is one of the most misunderstood tools in my toolbox — and one of the most effective for the right presentation.

A thin filiform needle is inserted into a taut band of muscle to elicit a local twitch response, which can reduce pain and restore normal muscle length. It is not acupuncture, though the needles look similar.

It tends to help most with stubborn trigger points that haven't responded to stretching or manual therapy alone — often in the neck, shoulders, hips, and calves.
