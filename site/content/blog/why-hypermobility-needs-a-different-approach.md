If you've been told to just "strengthen it" and the pain keeps coming back, you're not alone — and you're not doing it wrong. Hypermobile joints move through a larger range than a typical strengthening program accounts for, which means stability has to be built differently.

For many of my patients with hypermobility spectrum disorder or EDS, the missing piece isn't more exercise — it's _where_ in the range that exercise happens, how breath and bracing are cued, and how much the nervous system trusts the joint to begin with.

A few things I look at in almost every hypermobile evaluation:

- Where in the joint's range does control break down first?
- Is breath being used to create stability, or is it being held?
- Are we training the end-range, not just avoiding it?

None of this replaces a full evaluation, but if this sounds familiar, it might be worth a conversation.
