A lot of people come to their first appointment expecting the pace of a typical clinic visit — quick intake, a few questions, handed off to an aide. That's not how this works.

Your first visit is 100% one-on-one, in a quiet private space, and it starts with actually listening. We'll go through your history, what you've already tried, and what your goals are — not just for pain relief, but for what you want to get back to doing.

From there we move: a real movement assessment, hands-on treatment where it's useful, and a plan you leave with, not just a folder of handouts.
